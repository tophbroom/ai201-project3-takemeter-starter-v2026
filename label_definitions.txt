analysis: a point supported by a specific detail the poster actually gives as support (a number, date, named source, quote, link, or described mechanic) that the claim rests on; a fact that is merely asserted with no source or number, background facts, and numbers about the poster's own experience do not count
hot_take: a confident claim about a game, company, or the industry that someone could disagree with, with no support offered; rankings and comparisons count, and so do jokes or sarcasm that imply a claim about the world
reaction: the poster's own feeling, experience, plan, or question, or a pure joke that implies no claim, without an argued claim
